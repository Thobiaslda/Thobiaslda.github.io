Merger & employee comms

Building one employee voice through a merger

I rebuilt the communications function as two manufacturing businesses came together under one brand. Working directly with senior leadership and HR, I led employee communications through the transition, explained changes to employment arrangements and developed content and channels for factory and office teams. Internal communications received an 80% approval rating among employee survey respondents.

80%internal communications approval among survey respondents
2businesses brought together under one brand
How I supported the transition

Answer the questions behind the change. I developed employee communications covering contract continuity, benefits, employee records and the practical steps in the transition. I worked across leadership and HR to make sensitive changes clear and reduce uncertainty.

Rebuild the communication system. I diagnosed information silos and a largely top-down editorial approach, then authored an internal communications plan covering audience needs, content pillars, channels and measurement. I redesigned employee updates for mobile reading, standardised corporate materials and developed guidance on social media, confidentiality and authorised spokespeople.

Create the visual library. I photographed the company’s entire new image library, documenting its people, engineering, manufacturing processes and facilities for corporate communications.

Connect leadership, feedback and delivery. I led a three-person team and supported the launch of the code of conduct and reporting channel, coordinating across departments and suppliers. I also helped design the employee survey, presented findings to leaders and communicated results to staff. The plan proposed more employee stories, direct feedback and a stronger role for leaders in everyday communication.

Live crisis response

Responding to political pressure with an integrated campaign

When a mineral-royalty dispute put an industrial manufacturer under political and media scrutiny, I led a response spanning public affairs, media relations, employee communications and marketing. I analysed the political landscape, prepared leadership messages and helped plan a local campaign across outdoor media, radio and digital channels. Our response helped overcome the reputational crisis, gaining support from local authorities and community leaders as media and political pressure eased over two months.

Local supportgained from authorities and community leaders

How I coordinated the response

Understand the pressure. I mapped political alliances, influential local media and the way allegations were circulating through WhatsApp. That analysis informed the response priorities, leadership briefings and messages explaining the company's position on the disputed royalty assessment.

Build a campaign around evidence. Working with the advertising team, I helped plan outdoor billboards, LED panels, radio spots, local news advertising, social posts and WhatsApp content. I reviewed campaign copy built around employment, community programmes, environmental work and investment, using supporting figures to make the company's local contribution tangible.

Connect public and employee communication. The response plan paired press statements with a spokesperson Q&A, preparation for difficult questions and proposed meetings with local journalists. Employee messages explained the situation and reinforced the same factual points. Public affairs correspondence and community engagement formed part of the wider response.

Mining operation, Brazil

Crisis preparedness

Turning complex risks into decisions leaders can act on

I developed an integrated crisis management and communications plan for a mining operation in Brazil, connecting site leadership with its international corporate team. I mapped risks, assigned decision-making roles and built a first-hour response sequence covering fact verification, approvals, employee updates and public communication.

25risk scenarios mapped across operational and external threats
5detailed playbooks for incidents affecting people, communities and operations
How I built judgement into the response

Deciding when to speak. I built a visibility test around media enquiries, circulating images, visible signs of an incident and immediate community impacts. It gave the communications lead concrete grounds for recommending a holding statement. A timed approval process brought technical, legal and leadership review together, with an initial publication target within 60 minutes.

Putting people first. Five playbooks addressed serious injuries and fatalities, dam safety, environmental spills, misinformation and access blockades. They linked each scenario to immediate actions, affected audiences and draft messages. Family notification came before public identification, while community updates used accessible language and channels such as local radio and community leaders.

Keeping the response coordinated. Three escalation levels clarified when local and corporate teams became involved. Digital instructions covered pausing scheduled campaigns and automated replies, publishing verified updates and assessing rumours. Spokesperson briefings, reception scripts and a decision log supported consistent information across the response.

NZ organics sector

Digital & advocacy

Turning a complex policy issue into public action

As a volunteer for a national organics organisation, I developed digital content supporting its 2025 Gene Technology Bill campaign. I shaped clear calls to action for supporters to contact MPs, supermarkets and media, using video, carousels and social copy. Alongside the campaign, I audited the website and created a prioritised improvement plan covering audience relevance, mobile usability and search visibility.

3 routesfor supporter action through MPs, retailers and media
12 weeksplanning horizon for staged website improvements
How I shaped the campaign

Make participation practical. I developed captions and messages around specific actions, including emailing an MP, asking supermarkets about product choices and contacting local media. The campaign brief covered both the parliamentary submission period and continued engagement after the deadline, giving supporters ways to stay involved.

Give the issue a human story. My work included short video scripts, social stories and carousel content linking the policy debate to consumer choice, farming and the organic sector. I also developed ideas for farmer stories and member coverage to broaden the content beyond the immediate legislative campaign.

Use data to question the audience fit. I compared Ubersuggest, Semrush and SimilarWeb estimates, accounting for their different geographic and reporting periods. The audit flagged a potential mismatch between estimated international traffic and the intended New Zealand audience, alongside weak calls to action, mobile readability issues and slow-loading pages.

Turn the audit into priorities. I organised recommendations into immediate, four-week and twelve-week stages. These covered clearer membership and donation prompts, readable page layouts, better headings and internal links, image optimisation and more useful search content.

Elections in Brazil

Campaign strategy

Taking campaign intelligence from research to delivery

I led communications and coordinated a 50-person team for a winning Brazilian mayoral campaign. Across my electoral work, I used research and meetings with political leaders to shape positioning, recommend spending and staffing priorities, and adapt messages to local concerns. For a later state campaign, I analysed voting patterns across 13 municipalities in Power BI to inform campaign strategy.

47.5%of valid votes for the winning mayoral candidate
+83.5%more votes locally for the re-elected state candidate
How I worked on the campaigns

Turn research into a campaign direction. For the 2020 mayoral campaign, I analysed voter surveys, previous election results, candidate recognition and the issues residents prioritised. I used the findings to develop positioning and a costed communications plan, connecting the candidate's experience and proposals with local concerns.

Make decisions about resources. Across the campaigns, I helped determine where to recommend increased spending, where more staff were needed and which messages fitted each locality. Political meetings with leaders added local insight to the data and helped shape the campaign's direction.

Connect strategy with delivery. I led the mayoral campaign's communications and coordinated a 50-person team. Planning covered candidate interviews, biographies, policy proposals, social media profiles and WhatsApp distribution, giving the team a practical framework for consistent messaging.

Measure the electoral result. The mayoral candidate won with 47.5% of valid votes. In the 2022 state campaign, my Power BI analysis covered 13 municipalities. The re-elected candidate's vote in the focus municipality rose from 540 to 991, an 83.5% increase.
