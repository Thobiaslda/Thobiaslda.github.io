Expedição Piracicaba — River Conservation Campaign

Developed and executed the communications strategy for a scientific expedition down the Piracicaba river, moving through 21 cities across São Paulo state over several weeks. The goal was to turn a technical environmental research project into a public event that would generate political and community support for river conservation. Secured daily coverage on Rede Minas — Brazil's public television network, rebroadcast nationally — alongside print, radio, and local digital media. Every piece of coverage was positive in tone. Mayors, state legislators, and senior environmental scientists attended the community events that the media coverage helped mobilise.

Federal Highway Concession — Community & Stakeholder Communications

Four-year retained communications engagement for a 1,000 km federal highway concession spanning 34 municipalities across 3 Brazilian states. The central challenge: introducing paid tolls on a highway communities had always used for free. Rather than broadcasting top-down messaging, built a hyper-local engagement model — working directly with municipal journalists, mayors, community leaders, and politicians in each town to reframe the narrative from an imposition to a regional investment in safety and road quality. Achieved operational acceptance across all 34 municipalities without sustained organised opposition.
